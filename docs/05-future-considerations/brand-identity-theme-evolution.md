# Brand Identity and Theme Evolution

## Current Decision

Use the company-approved placeholder Meneer Health mark for v1 and retain the existing dark website
theme. Sprint 01.6 must solve the broken Lovable asset dependency without treating the placeholder
artwork as the final identity or redesigning established customer-facing presentation.

## Future Work

- Commission or approve a vector master and production-ready wordmark.
- Provide horizontal, stacked, mark-only, monochrome, light-background, dark-background, and
  high-contrast variants.
- Define clear space, minimum size, incorrect-use, colour, typography, and partner-lockup rules.
- Produce favicon, application icon, social-preview, document, poster, and print-ready exports.
- Review the dark theme against the final palette and test WCAG contrast at real rendered sizes.
- Decide whether the theme remains dark, becomes light, or supports both without weakening the
  established Meneer voice and visual character.
- Replace placeholder raster assets only after visual regression, responsive, accessibility, social
  preview, and representative print checks pass.

## Trigger

Revisit after pilot feedback or when final identity work is commissioned, and before public launch,
large-format campaign production, or the Next.js migration. This consideration is not authority to
change the current theme or messaging during unrelated engineering work.
