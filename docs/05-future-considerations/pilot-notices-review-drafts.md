## Draft A — Account and Pilot Terms

Meneer Health is an invitation-only pilot operated by [full contracting legal entity, registration
and contact address]. Octothorp ZA provides [confirmed technology/marketing/operations role and
contracted entity]. The pilot helps you provide account information, complete an intake questionnaire
and access the services specifically made available to you. Creating a profile is not a club-membership
purchase, a clinical diagnosis, a prescription or a guarantee of treatment or product availability.

You must meet the approved pilot eligibility requirements and provide accurate details. Keep access
codes private and notify us of suspected misuse. We may restrict access for security, safety or
eligibility reasons, subject to the approved complaints and rights process. We do not ask you to
send health information, access codes or bank/card details through ordinary support email.

Questionnaire submission itself is free. Where an approved clinical-review service is offered, its
exact price and applicable terms are shown before payment. The proposed R999 review deposit is
credited once up to the eligible first approved order's product subtotal; unused credit is refunded
to the original payment method. Delivery is separately priced. No payment guarantees a prescription
or removes a medical hold. These commercial paragraphs require approval of actual service, refund,
cancellation, tax, invoice and supplier arrangements before publication or real charges.

Contact [approved support address] for ordinary account queries. We aim to respond within 24 hours
where possible; this is not an emergency service. [Approved urgent-care instructions] apply to
urgent symptoms. Applicable statutory rights are not waived. [Approved complaints, applicable-law,
termination and refund wording] must be completed by the legal/commercial reviewer.

## Draft C — Medical Intake and Review Acknowledgement

This questionnaire provides information for review by an appropriately authorised healthcare
professional. It is not an emergency consultation, diagnosis or automatic prescription. Please
answer accurately and update us if relevant information changes. Optional blood-result documents
can support review; initial submission does not require blood results. The reviewing clinician may
request further assessment or tests before recommending treatment.

I understand the stated intake purpose, who will review my information, the applicable privacy
notice and the limitations of asynchronous communication. I understand that payment does not
guarantee treatment. [Clinician-approved risk/urgent-care guidance and professional identity].

Collection acknowledgement and any required consent must be recorded separately. A further
recipient-specific authorisation is required before manual external transfer; it must identify the
recipient, purpose, exact record/snapshot and information shared. This draft is not blanket consent
to disclose all health information, to prescribe, to perform treatment or to use records for marketing.
Any telehealth consultation and treatment consent must be separately reviewed for the actual service.
