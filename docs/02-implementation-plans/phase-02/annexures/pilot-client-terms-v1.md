### What the pilot provides

or club subscription. Access does not guarantee a consultation, prescription, product,

Use your own verified email and provide accurate information. The SMS invitation is personal:
do not forward it or register on another person's behalf. Invitations are valid for 48 hours
unless withdrawn or replaced; email verification codes expire after 15 minutes. An invitation
link alone is not independent proof of identity. Keep verification codes private and contact
support if access may have been misused. Staff may suspend access to investigate misuse,
security risks or safety concerns; suspension does not automatically forfeit money or waive
your rights to information, complaints or an eligible refund.

### Review, communication and changes

Questionnaire submission is not a diagnosis, prescription or treatment authorisation.
Clinical decisions require an appropriately authorised clinician; support or administrative
staff cannot make those decisions. Payment does not override a medical hold or authorise
dispensing. Product information and indicative availability are not advice to start treatment.

We aim to answer ordinary enquiries within 24 hours where possible. This is not a guaranteed
response time or an emergency service. For urgent or life-threatening symptoms, seek immediate
local emergency care; do not wait for a portal response, SMS or email.

We may improve pilot functionality, but material changes to terms or information handling
will be presented separately where acknowledgement or consent is required. They do not
silently change a price or accepted order. Your accepted document version and transaction
references are recorded. Contact support to obtain a copy of applicable terms or a transaction
record through an appropriately verified channel.

### Your rights and complaints

Raise account or payment complaints with support@meneerhealth.co.za and privacy complaints
with privacy@meneerhealth.co.za. South African mandatory consumer and privacy protections
remain applicable; these terms do not exclude statutory cooling-off, cancellation, refund,
defective-goods, information-access or complaint rights where they apply. We do not require
you to waive such rights or use an exclusive foreign forum to resolve a complaint.

Restrictions and erasure requests must be reconciled with provider and backup copies, and any
restore must reapply current restrictions. We do not promise instantaneous deletion of all copies.

The platform uses restricted access, encrypted medical content and encrypted recovery archives.
No service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your account access private and report suspected
misuse promptly without sending credentials or medical details by ordinary email.

### Access, correction, withdrawal and complaints

Contact privacy@meneerhealth.co.za to request access, correction, objection/restriction or deletion,
or to withdraw a consent where applicable. We may verify your identity before releasing or changing
records. Withdrawal may prevent a service that needs the relevant information; it does not erase

You may also raise a complaint with South Africa's Information Regulator at
https://inforegulator.org.za, enquiries@inforegulator.org.za or 010 023 5200.
