# Product order terms — review draft

This engineering draft restates the approved Sprint-11/15 commercial direction. It is not a
published customer instrument, legal opinion, professional agreement or product-sales approval.
The actual seller/collector particulars, professional/provider arrangements, delivery policy and
reviewer sign-off must be checked before publishing an immutable product-order instrument.

1. Your quote identifies the exact products, quantities, customer prices, product subtotal,
   review-deposit credit, separately charged delivery and the remaining amount payable. It also
   identifies its validity period and the version of these terms. A changed quote requires new
   review and acceptance; a previously accepted quote is not silently repriced.
2. The R999 review deposit is not a club membership or wallet balance. For the first approved
   product order in the review case, credit is capped at the product subtotal and does not reduce
   delivery charges. Any unused deposit is returned to its original payment method under the
   retained refund process. Do not pay the same confirmed transaction again.
3. Acceptance and payment do not prescribe treatment, confirm dispensing or guarantee supply or
   delivery. Current independently attributed clinical/provider evidence is required, and later
   safety, availability or authority changes may stop progression and require staff review.
   Required protocol-generator output is not assumed while that service is inactive.
4. The approved fulfilment direction is Precise Wellness to Meneer, then a separately recorded
   courier dispatch to the client. Actual delivery charges and arrangements must be confirmed in
   the quote; this draft invents no tariff, courier commitment or delivery deadline.
5. You may decline an unstarted product quote. This releases only unspent reserved credit; it is
   not itself a refund of an already captured payment. Once Checkout is being prepared, is open,
   or its outcome is uncertain, contact support for cancellation/reconciliation. Approved refund
   reasons use the original funding methods; unresolved no-show, late-cancellation and
   post-pharmacy-release exceptions remain staff reviewed, with no invented automatic forfeiture.
6. Keep clinical information out of ordinary payment messages. Hosted Checkout describes the
   approved order balance and delivery generically. Product/clinical details remain in the
   authenticated system and appropriately governed private provider records.
7. For help, use the existing approved support channel. Ordinary enquiries retain the agreed
   24-hour response target where possible; emergencies require immediate emergency care rather
   than waiting for email or an order review.

Publication must identify the actual contracting seller and confirmed collection arrangement:
OCTOTHORP ZA remains seller and Octothorp LLC collects under the owner's confirmed arrangement,
with Meneer Health branding. Use the existing authoritative company/contact particulars, not
invented registration, tax or professional details. Publication must be independently approved
and versioned; the database computes the exact content hash used by client acceptance.
